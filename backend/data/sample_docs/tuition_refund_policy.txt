1. Payment Deadlines
Tuition is due in full by the first day of classes each term. Students who have not paid
or enrolled in a payment plan by the tenth day of the term are assessed a $150 late fee
and may have a registration hold placed on their account.

2. Refund Schedule for Withdrawals
Students who withdraw from a course receive a refund based on the withdrawal date:
  - Before the first day of classes: 100% refund
  - During week 1: 90% refund
  - During week 2: 75% refund
  - During week 3: 50% refund
  - During week 4: 25% refund
  - After week 4: no refund
The refund schedule is based on the date the withdrawal form is received by the
registrar, not the date the student stops attending.

3. Payment Plans
The university offers a four-installment payment plan each term for a $45 enrollment fee.
Enrollment in a payment plan must be completed before the tuition due date. Missed
installments incur a $35 fee per occurrence.

4. Financial Aid Adjustments
Students receiving financial aid who withdraw before completing 60% of the term may be
required to return a prorated portion of their aid. The bursar's office recalculates aid
eligibility within 30 days of the withdrawal and bills the student for any balance owed.

5. Appeals
Refund exceptions for documented medical or military circumstances may be requested in
writing to the bursar's office within 90 days of the withdrawal date.
