1. Grade Scale
Undergraduate courses are graded A (4.0), A- (3.67), B+ (3.33), B (3.0), B- (2.67),
C+ (2.33), C (2.0), C- (1.67), D+ (1.33), D (1.0), and F (0.0). A grade of C- or
higher is required to satisfy a major prerequisite.

2. Incomplete Grades
A grade of "I" (Incomplete) may be granted only when a student has completed at least
70% of the coursework and is prevented from finishing by documented circumstances.
Incomplete work must be submitted within 12 months of the end of the term, or the
grade converts automatically to an F.

3. Pass/Fail Option
Students in good standing may elect the Pass/Fail option for up to two elective courses
per academic year. The election must be filed with the registrar by the end of the
fourth week of the term and cannot be reversed afterward. Pass/Fail courses do not
count toward major or minor requirements.

4. Grade Appeals
A student who believes a final grade was assigned in error must first contact the course
instructor within 30 days of the grade posting. If unresolved, the student may file a
formal appeal with the department chair within 60 days of the grade posting. Appeals
filed after 60 days are not considered.

5. Academic Standing
A cumulative GPA below 2.0 places a student on academic probation. Students on
probation for two consecutive terms are subject to academic dismissal and must petition
the Committee on Academic Standing for reinstatement.
