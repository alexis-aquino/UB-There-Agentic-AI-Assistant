1. Registration Windows
Registration opens by class standing: seniors and graduate students in week 10 of the
prior term, juniors in week 11, sophomores in week 12, and first-year students in
week 13. Students with a registration hold cannot enroll until the hold is cleared.

2. Add/Drop Deadlines
Courses may be added through the end of the second week of the term. Courses may be
dropped without a transcript notation through the end of the fourth week. A course
dropped between weeks 5 and 10 appears on the transcript as a "W" and does not affect GPA.
No drops are permitted after week 10.

3. Full-Time Status
Undergraduates must carry at least 12 credit hours to be considered full-time.
Graduate students must carry at least 9 credit hours. Dropping below full-time status
may affect financial aid eligibility, on-campus housing, and international student visa
status, and requires advisor approval.

4. Course Overload
Enrolling in more than 18 credit hours in a term requires a cumulative GPA of 3.25 or
higher and written approval from the student's academic advisor and the dean of the
student's college. Overload credits beyond 18 hours are billed at the per-credit rate.

5. Leave of Absence
Students in good standing may request a leave of absence of up to two consecutive terms
by filing a request with the registrar before the term begins. Students returning from
an approved leave retain their original catalog year and degree requirements.
