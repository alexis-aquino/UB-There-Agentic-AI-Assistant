Tips for a good voice
  • Quiet room, same mic, same distance (a hand's width) every session.
  • Read exactly what is written, in the calm, friendly tone you want the assistant to have.
  • Keep the level bar mostly in the middle. Red means clipping: back off.
  • Short sessions are fine; progress is saved after every clip.

Enough to train. In PowerShell, in the backend folder: wsl bash voice_training/train.sh (setup steps are in RUNNING.md).
